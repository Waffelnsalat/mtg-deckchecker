Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Overview

MTG Deckchecker processes decklists so it can validate cards, resolve card data, and generate deck analysis. The tool does not require an account.

Decklist data

When you submit a decklist, the server receives the deck text and any selected options such as commander, companion, secret commander, target bracket, or preferred strategy. This data is used to produce the analysis response.

External services

The app may request card or deck information from services such as Scryfall, EDHREC, Commander Spellbook, Recommander, Archidekt, or Moxfield. Those services may receive the relevant card names, commander names, or imported deck URL needed for the requested feature.

Reports and feedback

If you submit a feedback or bug report, the report may include your comment, the current decklist, and analysis context needed to reproduce the issue.

Cookies and advertising

The current app does not need user accounts. If advertising is enabled later, third-party ad providers such as Google may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. This policy should be reviewed before ads are activated.

Contact

For privacy questions or correction requests, use the contact options on the Contact page.
