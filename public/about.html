About

Commander deck reads without pretending the table is a spreadsheet.

MTG Deckchecker is an unofficial EDH and Commander analysis tool. It reads a decklist, resolves the cards, detects likely strategy packages, and turns the result into a practical overview of power, bracket fit, weaknesses, suggestions, and social pressure.

What it is for

The tool is meant for deck tuning, table expectation checks, and quick reviews before games. It is most useful when you want to understand why a deck reads faster, slower, saltier, or more commander-dependent than expected.

What it is not

It is not an official Commander rules product, tournament authority, or perfect power calculator. Commander is contextual. The same list can feel different across pods, metas, budgets, and mulligan habits.

How it helps

Instead of only assigning a single number, the analysis separates shell quality, strategy fit, interaction, resilience, win plan, weak matchups, and table salt. That makes the result easier to challenge and improve.
